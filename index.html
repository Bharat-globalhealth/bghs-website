Who we are

A dependable extension of your healthcare operations.

Bharat Global Health Solutions is an India-based healthcare outsourcing initiative focused on supporting US healthcare organisations, health plans and revenue cycle partners with specialised coding and operational services.

We aim to combine trained talent, clear workflows and measurable quality practices to help clients manage workload, strengthen documentation outcomes and support efficient revenue cycle operations.

Our Vision

To build a trusted, globally recognised healthcare outsourcing company known for dependable service, professional excellence and long-term partnerships.

Our Mission

To help healthcare organisations improve coding accuracy, operational efficiency and revenue-cycle performance through skilled professionals, quality-driven processes and secure delivery.

Our Values

Integrity, accuracy, accountability, continuous learning, confidentiality and respect for every client and patient.
